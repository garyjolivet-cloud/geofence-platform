Not connected
Garmin Instinct 2 / Crossover / 2X Solar — install the GPS Bridge Connect IQ app on the watch, start it before connecting here. The app streams GPS over Bluetooth UART (NUS) — no cable needed.

Other BLE GPS receivers — devices that broadcast the standard Location & Navigation Service (LNS / GATT 0x1819) connect automatically with no extra app.

Protocol is auto-detected on connect. Requires Chrome or Edge.

A dongle with a barometric altimeter (Elevation Source = Barometric, or the extra NUS altitude fields) improves accuracy for altitude-gated stops — otherwise altitude is estimated from terrain elevation at your GPS position when 3D Mode is on.

Before you start

This walk uses your location to know when you reach a stop. Your GPS never leaves your device unless you opt into Route Recording below. The tour audio is pre-recorded narration — your voice is never recorded. Nothing else is collected unless you turn it on.

Your name (optional) Shown on the guide's map when route recording is on.

Your device gets a random anonymous ID — not your name or contact info. Data is processed on servers in the United States (Cloudflare). You can change these choices or erase all data anytime under Privacy.

Ridge Quest

How to play

← Back to Ridge Quest

Ridge Quest turns a day on the mountain into a game. Your phone tracks the runs, chutes, bowls, ridges and trails you actually ride. Earn points, keep a daily streak, and see where you land on the leaderboard.

You play in a browser — there's nothing to install. Add the Ridge Quest page to your home screen for a full-screen, app-like feel.

1 Getting set up

  1. Pick your resort and map. If you followed a link or QR code from the resort you'll skip straight past this.
  2. Create an account with an email and password, or use Continue with Google. Your account is specific to that one resort.
  3. Read and accept the three notices — your data, risk & liability, and the Alpine Responsibility Code. These are required once, and again only if the wording changes.
  4. Check conditions — current temperature, wind, and recent snowfall for the top of the hill.

After that you land on the Home screen, where everything below happens.

! Ride safely

Skiing and riding carry real risk of serious injury. Looking at a screen while moving makes it worse.

  • Only look at your phone while stopped and off to the side of the trail, where you're visible from above.
  • Never check the app while in motion. Ridge Quest runs fine with your phone in a pocket — you don't need to watch it to score.
  • Obey all posted signs and closures. The game never routes you anywhere; where you go is your call.

2 Tracking your runs

  1. Choose your activity — SkiHikeXC SkiBikeDrive Your resort may only offer some of these. You can't change activity mid-run, so set it before you drop in.
  2. Tap "Start tracking" and allow location access when your browser asks. Keep the app open — a pocket is fine, but don't fully close the tab.
  3. Ride the run top to bottom. A crossing only counts once you've covered about half of that run's length — a quick clip across the edge of a chute while traversing won't log it.
  4. Tap "Stop tracking" when you're done for the day.

Reading the status line

While tracking, Home shows your last GPS fix and its accuracy (e.g. ±8m). After each run you'll see a coverage read-out — "Sunny Chute — 87% ✓ logged" or "— 22% (not enough of it covered to count)" — so a run is never silently dropped.

Standing still does nothing. The game needs real movement down a run — testing it from the lodge won't log anything, and that's expected.

How runs are classified

  • Your chosen activity drives it, with two automatic exceptions:
  • Lift rides are detected by riding up a lift line — they're logged as transport and earn no points.
  • A run the resort marked as a hiking-only trail always logs as a hike, even if you move quickly on it.
  • On skis, a run taken slowly enough that you were clearly skinning or walking logs as a hike, not a ski descent.

3 Your map & progress

"My map"

Shows the whole run network for your resort, colored by difficulty. Tap any run for its name, difficulty and length. If your resort has it enabled, the map tilts into 3D terrain.

"% explored"

The share of the resort's run network you've actually ridden or walked, lifetime — tracked automatically in the background while you're skiing with tracking on.

4 Points, streaks & the leaderboard

How points are earned

Ski / HikeVertical descended × run difficulty × run type

LiftNothing — it's just the ride up

Difficulty multiplies steeply for skiing: a green is ×1, blue ×1.5, black ×2, double-black ×3 per vertical metre. Chutes, bowls and ridges add a further bonus over an ordinary run.

Powder bonus ❄

Fresh snow in the last 24 hours multiplies ski and hike points for that day — up to 1.5× on a 30 cm-plus day. It's read from the resort's own snow report, so you can't fake it, and runs that earned it show a ❄ in your list.

Streak

Any day you log at least one run, hike or lift ride keeps your streak alive. Miss a day and it resets to zero.

Leaderboard

Switch between Today and Season, and between All and a single activity. Only your display name and points are visible to other players. Set your display name when you sign up.

5 Friends

  • Add friends by email, or share your personal invite link / QR code. Both of you play at the same resort.
  • "Share my location with friends today" turns on live location. It's strictly mutual — you only see a friend on a day you're both sharing — and it switches itself off every night.
  • Tick up to 6 friends in the Friends list to show them live on "My map" while you're both out and sharing.

6 Your data & account

  • GPS is collected only while a tracking session is active, and used to work out your runs, vertical, stats and map.
  • Location data is never sold.
  • Delete my account & data on the Home screen is a real, permanent deletion of your account and everything derived from your GPS history — not a deactivation.
  • Switch resort and Log out are also on the Home screen. Each resort is a separate account.

? If something's not working

My run didn't count

You probably didn't cover enough of it — aim to ride a run end to end with tracking on the whole way down. Check the accuracy figure in the status line too; poor GPS (large ± value) under heavy trees or cliffs can stop terrain from registering.

"Start tracking" seems to do nothing

If you're standing still, that's expected — move down a run. If you see a location error, open your browser's site settings and allow location for this page, then try again.

The map says it isn't available

The resort hasn't finished setting up its runs for Ridge Quest yet. Tracking and points still work; the map fills in once they do.

I forgot my password

Use Forgot password? on the login screen — you'll get a 6-digit code by email, good for 15 minutes.

Keep the Ridge Quest tab open and your screen unlocked periodically while tracking a long day — some phones aggressively suspend background tabs, which pauses GPS.

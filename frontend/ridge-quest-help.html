Ridge Quest

How to play

← Back to Ridge Quest

Ridge Quest turns a day on the mountain into a game. Your phone tracks the runs, chutes, bowls, ridges and trails you actually ride. Earn points, keep a daily streak, and see where you land on the leaderboard.

You play in a browser — there's nothing to install. Add the Ridge Quest page to your home screen for a full-screen, app-like feel.

1 Getting set up

  1. Pick your resort and map. If you followed a link or QR code from the resort you'll skip straight past this.
  2. Create an account with an email and password, or use Continue with Google. Your account is specific to that one resort.
  3. Read and accept the three notices — your data, risk & liability, and the Alpine Responsibility Code. These are required once, and again only if the wording changes.
  4. Check conditions — current temperature, wind, and recent snowfall for the top of the hill. Tap Start tracking right there to begin, or just look around to go to Home without tracking.

After that you land on the Home screen, where everything below happens.

! Ride safely

Skiing and riding carry real risk of serious injury. Looking at a screen while moving makes it worse.

  • Only look at your phone while stopped and off to the side of the trail, where you're visible from above.
  • Never check the app while in motion. Ridge Quest runs fine with your phone in a pocket — you don't need to watch it to score.
  • Obey all posted signs and closures. The game never routes you anywhere; where you go is your call.

2 Tracking your runs

  1. Tap "Start tracking" and allow location access when your browser asks. Keep the app open — a pocket is fine, but don't fully close the tab.
  2. Ride the run top to bottom. A run only counts once you've skied it from near its top to its bottom, covering about 80% of its length — a quick clip across the edge of a chute while traversing won't log it.
  3. Tap "Stop tracking" when you're done for the day.

Reading the status line

While tracking, Home shows your last GPS fix and its accuracy (e.g. ±8m). After each run you'll see a coverage read-out — "Sunny Chute — 87% ✓ logged" or "— 22% (not enough of it covered to count)" — so a run is never silently dropped.

No signal?

Runs still log. They're saved on your phone and sent when you have signal again. Under Start tracking, Home shows "📶 2 runs saved on your phone" while any are waiting, and "✓ All runs sent" when none are.

Standing still does nothing. The game needs real movement down a run — testing it from the lodge won't log anything, and that's expected.

How runs are classified

  • Ridge Quest is for skiing — there's nothing to choose. Every run is worked out for you:
  • Ski — skiing a run or chute downhill.
  • Boot pack Boot pack — climbing a route the resort marked as a boot pack. Only those routes count: going up a chute or a run isn't logged at all, and a run walked down too slowly to be skiing it isn't counted (you'll see why on Home). A boot pack never counts as a chute skied; what it does is add the height you climbed to your vertical.
  • Lift rides are counted too: Home shows today's lift rides; tap Lifts in the Leaderboard's You box for each lift and how many times you rode it, today or this season.
  • Vertical (today on Home, this season on the Leaderboard) is everything you went up — your lift rides plus your boot packs, from the resort's own lift and route elevations. Whatever you went up, you skied down.
  • Lift rides are detected by riding up a lift line — they're logged as transport and earn no points.

3 Your map & progress

Home

At the top is a 3D picture of the mountain with today's chutes in gold and your boot packs in green, with today's vertical, chutes, runs and lift rides over it (Runs are the runs you skied that aren't chutes; points are on the Leaderboard). To save your battery the picture isn't a live map: it's redrawn after each chute you finish, each time you get on the gondola at the bottom of the hill, and once at the start of each day. Under it: Start tracking and buttons for everything else. Your season numbers are on the Leaderboard. Your account settings are folded away at the bottom.

"My map"

Shows the whole run network for your resort, colored by difficulty. Tap ⓘ at the top for what each button does and tips for getting your runs and chutes logged. Tap any run for its name, difficulty and length. If your resort has it enabled, the map tilts into 3D terrain.

"Season" — your chutes and runs collection

The Season button on My map shows how many of the resort's chutes and runs you've skied this season (e.g. Season 38/132). Tap it to colour the map: chutes and runs you've skied turn gold, the ones you haven't yet turn grey. Lifts and boot packs keep their own colours. Tap Chutes in the Leaderboard's You box for the list: skied, and Not yet, for today or the season.

Your own names

Call a chute, run, boot pack or lift whatever you like: tap it on My map, then ✏️ Rename. Your name shows on your screens and the voice uses it ("This is …"). It's saved on your account, so it stays all season and on any phone. Only you see it: the leaderboard and shared images always use the resort's names. Tap ↩ Resort name to go back, or use ↩ Use the resort's names again on the chutes list (Leaderboard → Chutes) to reset them all.

"Skied"

The Skied button on the map shows a light stripe down every chute you've finished today, plus a thin line where you've skied (lift rides aren't drawn). Finish a chute and you'll see "Chute 7 of 64" with a gold flash. Tap the button to hide or show both.

🛡 Corridor Guard & the voice — best practice

Corridor Guard is the yellow 🛡 Guard button on My map. A guarded chute or run gets a yellow boundary line on the map, and your phone plays a tone if you drift outside that line.

The chime. Drop in at the top and ski 90% of a guarded chute or run, and you hear a short rising chime. From there the Guard is quiet for that run, so you can leave it wherever you like. The chime is not the log: a chute is logged when you reach its bottom.

The voice. While you're skiing inside a chute or run, Ridge Quest says the name of the run you're in, and of any chute or run you pass within about 5 m: "This is Cat Fight." Lifts are named when you get on. Only guarded runs speak, so the Guard button decides what you hear.

Guard ON — every chute speaks

With Guard ON, every chute and run is guarded, so every one you enter or pass is named. Many chutes sit close together or overlap, so you can get cross-talk: two names back to back, with the second cutting off the first, and tones from runs next to the one you're actually skiing.

  • Good for exploring and learning the names of the terrain around you.
  • To silence one run, press and hold it on My map (about 2 seconds) to mute it. Hold again to unmute.
  • To cut cross-talk, the resort may have set some overlapping chutes and runs to start off with Guard ON. They show greyed, without the yellow line. To hear one, press and hold it; hold again to turn it back off.

Guard OFF — only the runs you pick

With Guard OFF, nothing is guarded until you choose. Press and hold a chute or run on My map (about 2 seconds) to arm it. Only armed runs get the boundary line, the tone and the voice, and each one shows its name on the map. The button reads "Guard OFF · 2 armed". Hold an armed run again to disarm it.

  • Best for skiing a planned line. Arm the one or two chutes you're about to ski and you'll hear only those, with no cross-talk from neighbours.
  • While a run is armed, runs right beside it (within about 10 m) are ignored: they stay quiet and aren't logged. Disarm when you move on, so the rest of your day still counts.

Eyes up — the screen is black while you ski. Once you are moving at skiing pace the screen goes black, and touching it shows a warning instead of the map: looking at your phone while moving is dangerous. Stop somewhere safe and the screen comes back. It stays on while you ride a lift. Tracking, the tone and the voice keep working the whole time.

Set it up while stopped. Arm or mute runs at the top, standing off to the side, before you drop in. Never fiddle with the map while moving. The voice and tone are there so you don't have to look.

Share your day

Tap 📸 Social media export on Home. Pick Today or This season, and Story (Instagram / Facebook Stories) or Facebook post, then Make image. You get a 3D view of the mountain with your own lines on it, your vertical, chutes, runs, points, lift rides and the day's snow. Tap Save / Share to save it to your photos or post it straight from your phone's share sheet. The map needs a signal; without one the image uses mountain art instead.

4 Points, streaks & the leaderboard

How points are earned

ActivityScored on
Ski / Boot packVertical of the run × run difficulty × run type
LiftNothing — it's just the ride up

Difficulty multiplies steeply for skiing: a green is ×1, blue ×1.5, black ×2, double-black ×3 per vertical metre. Chutes, bowls and ridges add a further bonus over an ordinary run.

Powder bonus ❄

Fresh snow in the last 24 hours multiplies ski and boot-pack points for that day — up to 1.5× on a 30 cm-plus day. It's read from the resort's own snow report, so you can't fake it, and runs that earned it show a ❄ in your list.

Streak

Any day you log at least one run, boot pack or lift ride keeps your streak alive. Miss a day and it resets to zero.

Leaderboard

Switch between Today and Season. At the top, You — today shows your vertical, chutes and lift rides today; You — this season shows your season vertical, chutes skied (of the resort's total), lift rides and day streak. Tap Chutes or Lifts to see each one. Long lists scroll; Back stays at the bottom. Only your display name and points are visible to other players. Set your display name when you sign up.

5 Friends

  • Add friends by email, or share your personal invite link / QR code. Both of you play at the same resort.
  • "Share my location with friends today", at the top of the Friends screen, turns on live location. It's strictly mutual — you only see a friend on a day you're both sharing — and it switches itself off every night.
  • Tick up to 6 friends in the Friends list to show them live on "My map" while you're both out and sharing.

6 Your data & account

  • GPS is collected only while a tracking session is active, and used to work out your runs, vertical, stats and map.
  • Location data is never sold.
  • Delete my account & data on the Home screen is a real, permanent deletion of your account and everything derived from your GPS history — not a deactivation.
  • Switch resort and Log out are also on the Home screen. Each resort is a separate account.

? If something's not working

My run didn't count

You probably didn't cover enough of it — aim to ride a run end to end with tracking on the whole way down. Check the accuracy figure in the status line too; poor GPS (large ± value) under heavy trees or cliffs can stop terrain from registering.

"Start tracking" seems to do nothing

If you're standing still, that's expected — move down a run. If you see a location error, open your browser's site settings and allow location for this page, then try again.

The map says it isn't available

The resort hasn't finished setting up its runs for Ridge Quest yet. Tracking and points still work; the map fills in once they do.

I forgot my password

Use Forgot password? on the login screen — you'll get a 6-digit code by email, good for 15 minutes.

Keep the Ridge Quest tab open and your screen unlocked periodically while tracking a long day — some phones aggressively suspend background tabs, which pauses GPS.

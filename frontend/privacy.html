Privacy Policy

Chase Life Location Tours  ·  Effective date: July 8, 2026  ·  Governed by Canadian law (PIPEDA, BC PIPA)

Plain language first

Your GPS never leaves your phone unless you explicitly opt in to Route Recording. No account. No email. No name. A random anonymous ID is the only thing that links your choices together.

1. Who we are

Chase Life operates location-aware walking, skiing, and driving tours in British Columbia, Canada. We are the organization responsible for your personal information under PIPEDA and BC PIPA.

Privacy contact: gary.jolivet@gmail.com

2. What we collect and why

3. Cross-border data transfer

Our platform runs on Cloudflare Workers (servers in the United States). Any data you choose to share (stop events or route traces) is stored on US infrastructure. By opting in, you acknowledge this transfer. Cloudflare is bound by standard contractual protections and its own privacy commitments.

If you do not opt in to either optional collection, no personal data leaves Canada or your device.

5. Your rights

Under PIPEDA and BC PIPA you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of personal data we hold linked to your device ID
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — tap Forget this device & erase its data in the Privacy panel inside the app, or email us
  • Withdraw consent — reopen the Privacy panel anytime and uncheck options; future collection stops immediately
  • Lodge a complaint — with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca or BC's Information and Privacy Commissioner at oipc.bc.ca

To exercise any right by email, include the phrase "device ID request" and your anonymous device ID (visible in browser developer tools under localStorage key gp.device).

6. Children

This service is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please have a parent or guardian set your privacy choices. If you believe a child has provided personal information without consent, contact us to erase it.

7. Security

Data in transit is protected by TLS. Data at rest is stored in Cloudflare's D1 (SQLite) and R2 (object storage), both encrypted at rest. Access is restricted to authorized operators using scoped API keys.

8. Changes to this policy

When we make material changes, the consent screen inside the app will reappear and ask you to review the updated terms before continuing. The effective date above will be updated.
